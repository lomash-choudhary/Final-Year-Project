Expected answer

Foot rot usually also needs an antibiotic injection from the vet (ceftiofur, penicillin or a tetracycline). Call the vet if swelling spreads up the leg or she will not stand on the foot.

Expected answer

Wet, muddy conditions spread foot rot and digital dermatitis. A footbath of copper sulfate (3-5%) or formalin (3-5%) is used; oxytetracycline or lincomycin footbaths are an alternative. Dip each foot fully, keep floors and walkways dry and clean. Call the vet if cows become very lame or swelling spreads.

Call the vet if an udder becomes hot or swollen or the milk changes.
