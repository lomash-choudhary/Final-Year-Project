Dip each foot fully, keep floors and walkways dry and clean. Call the vet if cows become very lame or swelling spreads.

Expected answer

Dip every teat right after milking with a proven germicidal teat dip, covering at least the bottom three-quarters of the teat. Keep milking equipment working well, milk with clean hands or gloves, and keep udders clean and dry. Dry-cow treatment at drying-off also prevents mastitis. Call the vet if an udder becomes hot or swollen or the milk changes.
